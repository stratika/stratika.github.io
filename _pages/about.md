I am a Research Fellow at the University of Manchester with specialization on heterogeneous architectures and reconfigurable accelerators. My research interests include computer architecture, high-performance computing, virtualization and hardware acceleration across the computing continuum - from IoT and Edge devices to Cloud infrastructures. 

Currenlty, I am involved in the EU Horizon Europe [AERO](https://aero-project.eu/) as an innovation manager and the dissemination officer of the project which aims to bring up and optimize a software stack capable of operating on top of the emerging EU cloud processor.

In parallel, I am actively involved in the productization of [TornadoVM](https://www.tornadovm.org/), working to transition research outcomes into robust, industry-ready solutions.
